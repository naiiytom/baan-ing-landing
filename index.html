BAAN/ING incorporates structural rule algorithms aligned with Thailand's Building Control Act (พระราชบัญญัติควบคุมอาคาร พ.ศ. 2522) and Bangkok Metropolitan Administration (BMA) municipal bylaws. When you draft, the system automatically checks minimum ceiling clearance heights, setback distances from property boundaries, and minimum natural light/ventilation aperture ratios (min 10% of floor area for habitable rooms).

Our BOQ engine breaks down spatial geometries into metric material takeoffs (cubic meters of concrete, square meters of teak or tiles, linear meters of glass framing).

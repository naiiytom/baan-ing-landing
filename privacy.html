LEGAL & COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Last Updated: August 23, 2026 • Compliant with Thailand PDPA

1. Overview & Data Controller

BAAN/ING ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of architects, interior designers, and users accessing our architectural platform. This policy outlines how personal data and project telemetry are collected, processed, and secured in accordance with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA B.E. 2562).

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Data: Name, professional email address, studio role, and authentication credentials.
  • Project Telemetry: Floorplan dimensions, CAD metadata, BOQ itemization choices, and material preferences.
  • Technical Logs: IP address, browser user-agent, session durations, and render queue timestamps.

3. Intellectual Property & Blueprint Protection

Your architectural blueprints, CAD files, and bespoke designs remain strictly your intellectual property. BAAN/ING does not sell your floorplans or share private proprietary blueprints with third parties without explicit consent.

4. Data Retention & Security

All project data is stored in encrypted Cloudflare and AWS cloud environments utilizing AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. You may request complete deletion of your account and associated project data at any time.
